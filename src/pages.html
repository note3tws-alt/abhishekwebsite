Freelancer or agency? An honest comparison.

    
Read the full guide

  

  
+
Where a freelancer fits best
For most small and mid-sized Dubai businesses running one or two main channels.
  • You talk to the person doing the work, with no account managers in between
  • The person who plans the campaign also writes the scripts and runs the ads
  • You pay for the work, not for an office and a sales team
  • Flexible: a project, a month at a time, or a few hours of consulting

  
−
When an agency is the better choice
I’ll tell you if this is you.
  • You need a high volume of designed assets and video every week
  • You run TV, outdoor, PR and digital together
  • You need several specialists working at the same time in several markets

  
{{i:team}}The common middle ground: I lead the strategy and the paid campaigns, while your designer, a videographer or editors I coordinate produce the assets to a brief. That is how I worked with the Modus D3 creative team.
